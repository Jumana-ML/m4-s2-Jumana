Finding Summary:
An analysis of the Average Order Value (AOV) reveals that the "Books" category leads the market with an AOV of 120.50 JOD, unexpectedly outperforming "Electronics" (116.00 JOD). While initially counterintuitive, a deep-dive data validation confirms this accuracy. This dominance is driven by our "highest-value item" classification methodology; customers frequently purchase high-ticket, specialized academic texts. When these expensive books are in a cart, they financially dominate the transaction, categorizing the entire order under "Books" even if cheaper electronics are present. This finding validates our data integrity and highlights a highly profitable, premium customer segment within Books, warranting targeted marketing investments.
